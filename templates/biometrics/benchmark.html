How the 3-Factor Security System Works
1
Stage 1: Password (What You Know) Your unique student password secures your account from unauthorized browsing.
2
Stage 2: OTP Passcode (What You Have) A 6-digit dynamic code confirms you have your personal authenticator device.
3
Stage 3: Fingerprint (Who You Are) Your physical biometric scan confirms you are present in person at the terminal. Even if someone steals your password, they cannot vote for you.
